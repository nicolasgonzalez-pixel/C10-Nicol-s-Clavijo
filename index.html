Show the regret table
How are they calculated?
  • Max (Maximax): the highest payoff of each alternative. Choose the largest. Optimistic attitude.
  • Min (Maximin): the lowest payoff of each alternative. Choose the largest minimum. Pessimistic or conservative attitude.
  • Regret: in each state, the best payoff in that column minus the alternative's payoff. Max regret is the largest regret in the row; Minimax Regret chooses the smallest.
  • EMV / Bayes: sum of payoff × probability over all states. Choose the largest. Requires probabilities that add up to 1.
  • Ties: two values are tied if they differ by less than 10⁻⁹. Values are shown with 2 decimals but calculated at full precision.

Step 5 · Staged filtering

Eliminate alternatives step by step.

Rules are applied in order, and each one uses only the survivors of the previous stage. Eliminated alternatives never return. If there is a tie at the cutoff, all tied alternatives survive.

Tip: use “keep 1” in the last stage to get the final selection.
